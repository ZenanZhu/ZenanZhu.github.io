# Images

Add your chosen portrait as `portrait.jpg` and configure its `src` in `assets/js/media.js`. The circular portrait crop is controlled by `.portrait-slot > img` in `assets/css/style.css`.

Research figures may be PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG. Configure the matching project media key in `assets/js/media.js`. Use only images you are authorized to publish. No portrait or research images are included automatically.
